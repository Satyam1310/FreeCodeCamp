Travel Jamshedpur

Jamshedpur, located in Jharkhand, is India's first planned industrial city, founded by Jamsetji Tata in the early 20th century. Known as "Steel City" or Tatanagar, it serves as the headquarters for Tata Steel and is renowned for its wide roads, lush greenery, industrial development, and high quality of life.
